# Cantonese Claypot Rice with Chinese Sausage and Shiitake (煲仔饭)

## Description
A Cantonese one-pot rice dish in which jasmine rice is steamed with Chinese sausage, shiitake mushrooms, and ginger, then finished with a savory soy drizzle and a crisp rice crust. A heavy-bottomed lidded pot can be used if a clay pot is unavailable, though the crust may be lighter.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 2 cups jasmine rice, rinsed, soaked for 20 minutes, and drained
- [ ] 2 1/4 cups water
- [ ] 2 teaspoons neutral oil
- [ ] 2 Chinese sausages (lap cheong), thinly sliced on the diagonal
- [ ] 5 ounces fresh shiitake mushrooms, stems removed and caps sliced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (or vegetarian mushroom sauce)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon dark soy sauce
- [ ] 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- [ ] 1 teaspoon sugar
- [ ] 2 tablespoons water
- [ ] 2 scallions, thinly sliced

### Steps for preparation:

1. Rinse the rice until the water is mostly clear. Soak it in fresh water for 20 minutes, then drain well.
2. In a small bowl, stir together the light soy sauce, oyster or mushroom sauce, dark soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and 2 tablespoons water. Set aside.
3. Put the drained rice, 2 1/4 cups fresh water, and neutral oil in a clay pot. Cover and bring to a gentle boil over medium-low heat; once boiling, reduce the heat to low and cook until most of the water has been absorbed and the surface of the rice looks nearly dry, about 10–12 minutes.
4. Arrange the sausage, mushrooms, and ginger over the rice. Cover and cook on low for 10–12 minutes, until the rice is tender and the mushrooms are cooked through. If the pot becomes dry before the rice is tender, carefully add 1–2 tablespoons hot water around the edge.
5. Turn off the heat and let the covered pot rest for 10 minutes. Drizzle the sauce over the rice and scatter the scallions on top.
6. Serve hot from the pot, scraping up some of the browned rice crust with each portion.

### Notes:

- If using a traditional clay pot, heat it gradually and avoid sudden temperature changes. A lidded, heavy-bottomed saucepan works as a substitute.
- For a vegetarian version, omit the Chinese sausage, use vegetarian mushroom sauce, and add browned firm tofu.
- Contains pork, soy, and sesame; soy sauces commonly contain wheat, and oyster sauce contains shellfish. Check package labels for exact allergens.
